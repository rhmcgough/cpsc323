## Exercise 3
FixedUpdate() ties updating to a timing variable, such as framerate, to ensure that physics or other real time rendering can happen in an intentional timetable. FixedUpdate creates a rate of which something should be updating at, rather than Update() that allows things to occur without any form of timing.

## Exercise 8
Keeping health as a public field is risky, it allows other scripts to change its value, allowing for unintentional bugs that could be anywhere in the program. [Serialize Field] allows the value of health to be editable in the inspector, but not by any other scripts, encapsulating the script so that only the programmer can intentionally change it.

The error states what the null reference is and where it traces to.
